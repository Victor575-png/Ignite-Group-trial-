SEO Basics: How Small Businesses Get Found on Google

By the Ignite Group Team · SEO & Web · 7 min read

SEO (search engine optimization) sounds technical, but at its core it's simple: it's the set of things you do so that when someone searches for what you offer, Google shows your business instead of — or alongside — your competitors. Here's where to start, even with no budget.

Step 1: Claim your Google Business Profile

This is the single highest-impact, lowest-cost step available. It's the listing that shows your business on Google Maps and in local search results, with your hours, location, phone number and reviews.

  • Search "Google Business Profile" and set one up if you haven't.
  • Fill in every field — category, hours, service area, photos. Incomplete profiles rank lower.
  • Keep it updated when hours or details change.

Step 2: Use the right keywords naturally

Keywords are simply the words people actually type when searching. A bakery in Kikuyu should have phrases like "cakes in Kikuyu" or "birthday cakes Kiambu" naturally in their website text — not stuffed in unnaturally, just written the way a real customer would search.

Quick way to find keywords: Start typing a search into Google related to your business and look at the autocomplete suggestions — those are real phrases real people search for.

Step 3: Make your website mobile-friendly and fast

Most searches in Kenya happen on a phone. If your site is slow to load or hard to read on a small screen, people leave within seconds — and Google notices that and ranks the page lower as a result.

Step 4: Get listed and reviewed

Ask happy customers to leave a Google review. A handful of genuine, recent reviews does more for local ranking and trust than almost anything else on this list — and it costs nothing but a follow-up message.

Step 5: Publish helpful content regularly

This is exactly what this blog is — content that answers real questions your customers are already searching for. Every helpful post is another page Google can match to a search, and another reason for someone to trust your business before they've even spoken to you.

What SEO is not

It's not a one-time fix, and it's not instant — most businesses see meaningful movement over weeks and months, not days. It rewards businesses that stay consistent, which is exactly why most small businesses that try it briefly and give up never see the results they were hoping for.
